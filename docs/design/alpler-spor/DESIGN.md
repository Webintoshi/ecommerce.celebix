# Alpler Spor — Deniz frontend

## Direction

Premium urban sportswear, monochrome surfaces, Barlow Condensed 700 display typography and Inter body typography. Real catalog photography remains the source for products and the two large Giyim / AYAKKABILAR collection tiles. A single atmospheric campaign introduces the range without making shipping, authenticity or promotional promises.

The mobile experience has persistent five-item navigation, searchable real products, category navigation and Back-aware purchase sheets. Reduced-motion and keyboard focus are supported. Only Alpler's trusted tenant UUID activates this presentation.
