# Login helper artwork

The approved “Sempatik yardımcı” concept is implemented with real server-rendered controls and a separate decorative illustration. The illustration was extracted with the built-in image generation tool; no browser animation or 3D runtime is used.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: production illustration for an approved merchant login page. Edit target: attached approved full UI mockup. Extract ONLY the right-hand matte 3D scene as a standalone illustration with genuine transparent background around the scene. Preserve the exact friendly cream robot with nonglowing black eyes, smile, orange ear detail and tiny orange x on its chest, cream parcels with modest orange tape, paper shopping bag, pale wooden packing counter, plants, cream arched wall, shelving and pendant lamp. Keep the approved proportions, pose, camera angle, materials, quiet warm lighting and small orange accents. Frame the right scene naturally, comfortably including the robot and parcels. Remove all left-hand login UI, store monogram, all words, login buttons, Celebix top-left logo, dividers and trust icon. No text, no extra characters, no new objects, no neon, no glow, no strong shadow. Surrounding blank canvas must be transparent, not white, while keeping interior cream architectural surfaces. This will be composited beside real HTML controls on #f8f7f5.
